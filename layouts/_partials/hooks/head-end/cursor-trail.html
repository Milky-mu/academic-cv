{{/* Glowing cursor with a fading light trail. A full-screen canvas sits above the page (it ignores clicks) and draws a soft halo that follows the pointer, plus a trail of light along its recent path that thins and fades out over TRAIL_LIFE ms. The colour comes from the theme's primary colour, so it follows theme.colors.primary. Skipped on touch screens and for visitors who ask for reduced motion. Why it looks smooth: the trail is not drawn through the raw mouse positions, which arrive unevenly and would give a jagged polyline. Instead a "head" eases towards the pointer every animation frame and the trail records the head, so its path is a smooth curve. The trail is then painted as a dense row of soft glow dots rather than stroked lines, so there are no hard edges or corners, and each dot fades on an ease-out curve instead of linearly. */}}
